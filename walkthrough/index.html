Floss Fox tester walkthrough

A guided tour of each page and feature, with a short questionnaire at the end of every section.

Thank you for helping test Floss Fox. Floss Fox is a free helper for cross-stitchers. It turns DMC color lists into matching brands like Anchor, keeps track of the threads you own, checks your projects against your stash, and helps you work out fabric and shopping.

Please do the walkthrough in the live app:

https://flossfox.com/

Your threads and projects are saved only on your own device. Nothing is sent to us. This walkthrough page does not save any of your answers and does not add trackers.

It takes about 20 to 30 minutes. Do the sections in order if you can. There are no wrong answers. If you get stuck, that is useful to know too, so just note it and move on.

Your helper along the way is Pip, a little fox. Pip uses they/them pronouns. They will show tips as you go. If you ever want them quieter, look in Settings for Pip's talking level.

How to send your answers

After each section, tap Copy my answers, then paste the text in a message or email to Myke. You can send one section at a time or collect them and send them all at the end.

1. Home

What it does: The first screen is a short welcome from Pip, with three main choices so you can get started without hunting through menus.

Try this

  1. Open https://flossfox.com/.
  2. Read Pip's greeting.
  3. Look at the three main choices: Find colors for a pattern, Start or continue a project, and Organize my threads.
  4. If you already have a project in progress, check whether a Continue button appears.
  5. Look for the quieter link to Pip's Den. You do not have to go in yet.
  6. Open More tools and see that Shopping, Fabric, Settings and Pip's Den live there.

2. Find floss

What it does: Turns a list of DMC color numbers into matching Anchor and other brands. You can paste a list or import a pattern PDF.

Try this

  1. Choose Find colors for a pattern, or open Find floss from the app.
  2. Type or paste a few DMC numbers, for example: 310, 699, 740, 3354, BLANC, WHITE, B5200.
  3. Look at the matches under the colors Floss Fox found. BLANC, WHITE and B5200 should stay as three separate colors.
  4. If you have a pattern PDF, try Import a pattern (PDF) and look at the colors Pip found. Fix anything Pip asks you to check. If you do not have a PDF, skip this step and say so below.
  5. If you want, add a color you own to your collection, or save the list as a project.

Notice: Were the matches easy to read? Did any words confuse you? Floss Fox suggests checking a real shade card before buying, since matches are close but not always exact.

3. My threads and Smart Stash

What it does: My threads is your keepsake box of the floss you already own. Smart Stash helps you fill that box faster: import a list, pick lots of colors at once, photograph labels, or scan barcodes. Photos and barcodes are read on your device and are not uploaded. Photo recognition and barcode scanning are free for now.

Try this

  1. Open Organize my threads, or go to My threads.
  2. Add one or two colors by hand, with how many skeins you have. Change one amount.
  3. In Smart Stash, try Import a list with a short typed or pasted list, or a file if you have one. Review the colors before you save, then try Undo if you want to put things back.
  4. Try Choose colors (bulk pick) and add a color that way.
  5. Try Photograph your threads if your device has a camera, or pick a photo of a skein label. Review any suggested numbers before saving. You can skip this if you prefer.
  6. Try Scan barcodes if you have a skein with a barcode handy. You can skip this too.

Notice: Was adding and changing threads easy? Did the review-before-saving step feel safe? Would you keep your real stash here?

4. Projects and the workbasket

What it does: A project holds one pattern's colors so you can mark Have enough, Need to buy, or Not sure, compare with your stash, and leave a note about where you left off.

Try this

  1. Open Start or continue a project, or go to Projects.
  2. Open a project (or save a new one from a short color list).
  3. For each color, choose Have enough, Need to buy, or Not sure. If you are offered a comparison with your collection, look at the preview first. Nothing should change until you confirm. There should be a way to Undo.
  4. Add a Where I left off note, for example "sky, one more row."
  5. If you like, add a cover photo of the project. This stays on your device.
  6. Look at your project list and the workbasket or project page to see whether the note and photo show up clearly, even if the project name is long.

Notice: Did the compare step feel safe? Was the note easy to find later?

5. Shopping

What it does: Gathers the floss you still need into a shopping list, with links to stores.

Try this

  1. Open More tools, then Shopping.
  2. Look at the shopping list for your project.
  3. If you see Kit this project, try it to set aside threads you already have. Look first, then confirm.
  4. Tap a color's store link to see where you could buy it. You do not need to buy anything.
  5. Try Copy as text or print the list if those options are there.

Notice: Would you use this list at the store or when ordering online?

6. Fabric calculator

What it does: Works out how much fabric to cut for a design.

Try this

  1. Open More tools, then Fabric.
  2. Enter your design size in stitches.
  3. Pick your fabric type and count, for example 14 count Aida, and how much extra fabric you want around the edges.
  4. Look at the finished design size and the fabric to cut, in inches and centimeters.

7. Pip's Den

What it does: Pip's Den is an optional corner for stitching time and memories. It does not replace your projects or your thread collection. Everything here stays on your device.

Try this

  1. Open Pip's Den from the home link, or from More tools.
  2. Stitchside: start a short session on a project, then pause or end it.
  3. Pip's Treasures: save a discovery if one is offered, and try Undo if you want to take it back.
  4. Stitching Journey: add a short note, and a photo if you like, about a moment in your project.
  5. Keepsake Collection: look for a finished project. Open it if one is there.
  6. Stitch and Share: if a postcard download is offered, try it. It should save a picture on your device and not post anywhere.

Notice: Did the Den feel like a nice extra, or did it get in the way of the main tools?

8. Backup and restore

What it does: Because your data lives only on your device, a backup file lets you keep a safe copy or move it to another device. Floss Fox also keeps automatic copies on this device that you can restore.

Try this

  1. Open More tools, then Settings, and find Backup.
  2. Download a backup and save the file somewhere you can find it.
  3. Look at Automatic copies on this device and the Restore list. Just look if you prefer. If you do try a restore, make sure you have a downloaded backup first.
  4. Optional, only if you are comfortable: restore from the file you just downloaded onto this same device, and check that your projects and threads come back as they were.

9. Settings, themes and Help

What it does: Settings is where you can change how Floss Fox looks, how talkative Pip is, and find Help.

Try this

  1. Open More tools, then Settings.
  2. Try a theme if you see theme choices (for example a lighter or darker look). You can switch back.
  3. Look at Pip's talking level if it is there, and change it if you want.
  4. Open Help and skim how Floss Fox works, or what the words mean.
  5. Look at Recent changes if it appears, just to see what it tells you.
